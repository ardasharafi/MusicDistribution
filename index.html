What you'll need

Have these ready before you start the form.

  • Cover art: square JPG, at least 3000 × 3000 pixels
  • Audio: WAV files (16-bit or 24-bit, 44.1 kHz or higher)
  • Artist name and your full legal name
  • Track titles and credits (composer, producer, engineers)
  • Your Spotify and Apple Music artist links, if you already have them
