Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

What happens after I submit my release?

Our team reviews your metadata, artwork and audio. If anything needs to be fixed, we email you. Once everything is approved, we deliver your release to the stores for your chosen release date.

How far in advance should I submit?

We recommend submitting at least 3 weeks before your release date. This leaves time for review, store processing and pitching to playlists. Earlier is always better.

What are the cover art requirements?

A square JPG image of at least 3000 × 3000 pixels. Artwork must not contain website URLs, social media handles, store logos, pricing or blurry or pixelated images. The text on the cover should match the artist name and release title.

What audio format do I need?

Upload uncompressed WAV files, 16-bit or 24-bit, at 44.1 kHz or higher. MP3 and other compressed formats are not accepted by most stores.

Can I release an EP or an album?

Yes. Choose EP or Album as the release type on the submission form and upload one WAV file per track. List the track names in release order.

I don't have a UPC or ISRC. Is that a problem?

No. Leave those fields empty and codes will be assigned to your release. If you already have codes from a previous distributor, enter them so your release keeps its history.

Why do you need my legal name and date of birth?

Stores and rights organisations require the real identity of the rights holder and composer. Your legal name is used for songwriter credits and royalty records. It is not shown publicly as your artist name.

This is my first release. What do I put for Spotify and Apple Music links?

Leave them empty. New artist profiles are created when your first release goes live. If you already have profiles, add the links so the release goes to the right artist page.

Can I use samples or cover songs?

Only if you have cleared the rights. Releases with uncleared samples can be removed by stores. For cover songs, mention it in the notes field so we can handle it correctly.

Can I change my release after it's live?

Some details can be updated after release. Contact us with your release title and the changes you need.
